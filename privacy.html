Privacy Policy

Last updated: 16 November 2026

What data is used

When you connect TikTok or Instagram, Clip Studio receives an access token and your basic profile information (account id, display name). It uses them only to upload the videos you choose to your own account and to show which account is connected.

Where it is stored

Tokens and all videos stay on your own computer, in the application's local configuration file. Clip Studio has no servers; nothing is sent to the author or to any third party other than the platform you publish to.

Sharing

Your data is not sold, shared or used for advertising.

Deleting your data

Disconnect the account in Clip Studio settings or revoke access in your TikTok / Instagram settings (Security → Manage app permissions). Deleting the local configuration file removes all stored tokens.
