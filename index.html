Clip Studio

Clip Studio is a personal desktop tool that turns gaming livestream highlights into short vertical videos (captions, cropping, music) and lets its owner upload them to their own TikTok and Instagram accounts.

Clip Studio je osobní nástroj, který z highlightů herních streamů dělá krátká svislá videa a umožňuje jejich majiteli nahrát je na vlastní účty TikTok a Instagram.

The app runs locally on the owner's computer. It has no user accounts, no servers and does not collect data from other people.
